THC:CBD ratio and chronic pain: balanced or high-THC gave small drops; low-THC/high-CBD may not help

In plain words

A 2026 systematic review pooled 25 trials with 2,303 people, mostly using pills and sprays.

Balanced and high-THC products gave small pain drops, about 0.5 to 0.8 points on a 10-point scale, with more dizziness, sedation and nausea.

Low-THC/high-CBD products may not help.

Typical strains

Typically balanced: Harlequin, Pennywise. Check the COA.

Studies (PubMed)
  • SRChou et al., Ann Intern Med 2026 PMID 41429020 · DOISystematic review of 25 RCTs (n=2,303). High-THC −0.78, comparable THC:CBD −0.54 pts (0–10); low THC:CBD may not help; more dizziness/sedation/nausea.

Studies suggest; they do not prove. Animal results may not apply to people. Not medical advice; talk to a doctor.
