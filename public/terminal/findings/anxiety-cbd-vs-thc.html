Latest findings · Anxiety + mood · 2026-10-29

CBD vs THC for anxiety: CBD and balanced flower groups improved far more than THC-only

Human naturalistic study (no placebo) · meta-analyses mixed AI-graded ⓘ

AI-graded: the grade and plain-words summary were written by AI from the cited studies; PubMed and DOI links were checked automatically. Not reviewed by a doctor. Always check the source.

Share on XSee Anxiety + mood in the Scientist
In plain words

345 adults using cannabis for anxiety were randomly assigned THC, CBD or both for 30 days. In the flower groups, anxiety dropped 7.8% with 24% THC, 39.5% with 12% THC / 12% CBD, and 34.8% with 24% CBD.

The catch, graded honestly: no placebo, not blinded, and people used as much as they liked. Anxiety fell in every group. The authors call it associational, not proof.

The bigger picture is mixed. A 2026 meta-analysis (Psychiatry Research) found cannabinoids lowered anxiety symptoms in trials, with very low certainty. A 2026 meta-analysis of 54 RCTs (Lancet Psychiatry) found no significant effect on anxiety outcomes.

Mood and depression: neither 2026 review found a benefit, and Lancet Psychiatry found no RCTs that treated depression as the main target. In mice, CBD + β-caryophyllene (a peppery terpene) eased pain and depression-like behavior (Dec 2025). Mice only.

High THC can raise anxiety.

Typical strains

Typical picks: CBD-dominant or balanced. ACDC, Ringo's Gift (high CBD); Harlequin, Cannatonic (balanced). Check the COA.

Studies (PubMed)
  • HUMAN-OBSRosa et al., IJERPH 2026 PMID 41752306 · DOINaturalistic 30-day study, n=345, randomized product groups, no placebo. Flower anxiety drop: THC 7.8%, CBD 34.8%, THC+CBD 39.5%.
  • SRBharat et al., Psychiatry Res 2026 PMID 42617303 · DOISystematic review/meta-analysis. Anxiety SMD −0.40 in RCTs (very low certainty); trivial-to-no effect on depression.
  • SRWilson et al., Lancet Psychiatry 2026 PMID 41856154 · DOIMeta-analysis of 54 RCTs. No significant effect on anxiety outcomes; no RCT evidence for depression.
  • PRECLINICALRios et al., Biomedicines 2025 PMID 41463111 · DOIMouse study. Oral BCP + CBD synergistic vs neuropathic pain, antidepressant-like effect.

Studies suggest; they do not prove. Animal results may not apply to people. Not medical advice; talk to a doctor.

More findings
  • Sleep: CBN and sleep: a high dose helped people fall asleep faster, but did not cut night waking
  • Chronic pain: THC:CBD ratio and chronic pain: balanced or high-THC gave small drops; low-THC/high-CBD may not help
  • Migraine: THC + CBD flower vs migraine: 67% got relief at 2 hours, vs 47% on placebo
  • Inflammation / arthritis: β-caryophyllene and arthritis: it cut swelling in rats, while a THC pill didn't beat placebo for knee pain
  • Appetite: CBD and appetite: healthy adults ate 193 kcal more after CBD, without feeling hungrier
