Latest findings · Sleep · 2026-10-29

CBN and sleep: a high dose helped people fall asleep faster, but did not cut night waking

Human RCT (n=20) · mice for myrcene

Share on XSee Sleep in the Scientist
In plain words

A 2026 randomized trial gave 20 adults with insomnia single nights of CBN (the "sleepy" cannabinoid) at 30 mg, 300 mg and placebo, tracked in a sleep lab. The main goal, less waking during the night, did not change.

At 300 mg (vs placebo) people fell asleep faster, had more light (N2) sleep and rated their sleep better. 30 mg did nothing significant. 300 mg is a big dose, one night each, 20 people; the authors say larger, longer trials are needed.

THC + CBD: a 2025 pilot RCT (20 adults with insomnia) found one oral dose of 10 mg THC + 200 mg CBD cut total sleep by about 25 minutes and REM sleep by about 34 minutes vs placebo, with no next-day driving or thinking hit, just slightly more sleepiness. "Knocked out" isn't better sleep.

The terpene angle: in October 2026, inhaled β-myrcene helped insomnia-model mice fall asleep faster and sleep longer. Mice only.

Bottom line: CBN has an early human signal at high doses, THC can cut REM, and myrcene is mouse data so far.

Typical strains

Myrcene-forward strains typically: Granddaddy Purple, Northern Lights, Bubba Kush. Names vary; check the COA.

Studies (PubMed)
  • RCTLavender et al., J Sleep Res 2026 PMID 41698831 · DOIHuman crossover RCT, n=20 insomnia. 300 mg CBN: faster sleep onset, more N2, better self-rated sleep. Missed primary outcome (WASO).
  • RCTSuraev et al., J Sleep Res 2025 PMID 40631525 · DOIPilot human RCT, n=20 insomnia. 10 mg THC + 200 mg CBD: −24.5 min total sleep, −33.9 min REM vs placebo. No next-day driving/cognitive change.
  • PRECLINICALXu et al., Nutr Neurosci 2026 PMID 42858151 · DOIMouse study. Inhaled β-myrcene shortened sleep latency and lengthened sleep in insomnia-model mice.

Studies suggest; they do not prove. Animal results may not apply to people. Not medical advice; talk to a doctor.
