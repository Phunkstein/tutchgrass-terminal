Latest findings · Migraine · 2026-10-29

THC + CBD flower vs migraine: 67% got relief at 2 hours, vs 47% on placebo

Human double-blind RCT (n=92)

Share on XSee Migraine in the Scientist
In plain words

A 2026 double-blind crossover trial had 92 adults vape flower during migraine attacks (247 attacks treated).

6% THC + 11% CBD flower: 67% had pain relief at 2 hours, vs 47% on placebo.

THC-only flower gave relief but no edge on being pain-free. CBD-only flower was no better than placebo.

Typical strains

Typically CBD-leaning ~1:2 THC:CBD cuts. Check the COA.

Studies (PubMed)
  • RCTSchuster et al., Headache 2026 PMID 41469488 · DOIDouble-blind crossover RCT (92 adults, 247 attacks). 6% THC + 11% CBD flower: 67.2% vs 46.6% pain relief at 2 h; CBD-only no better than placebo.

Studies suggest; they do not prove. Animal results may not apply to people. Not medical advice; talk to a doctor.
