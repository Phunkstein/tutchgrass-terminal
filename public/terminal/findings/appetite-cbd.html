CBD and appetite: healthy adults ate 193 kcal more after CBD, without feeling hungrier

Small human RCTs (n=15, n=17) AI-graded ⓘ

AI-graded: the grade and plain-words summary were written by AI from the cited studies; PubMed and DOI links were checked automatically. Not reviewed by a doctor. Always check the source.

Share on XSee Appetite in the Scientist
In plain words

A 2026 crossover RCT: after about 300 mg of CBD isolate, 15 healthy adults ate 193 kcal more at a buffet lunch than on placebo (979 vs 786 kcal), without feeling hungrier.

But in 17 older adults (65+) with poor appetite, a THC + CBD spray (Sativex) didn't change appetite or appetite hormones (GLP-1, ghrelin).

Studies (PubMed)
  • RCTJohnson et al., Appetite 2026 PMID 41825697 · DOICrossover RCT (n=15 healthy). 298 mg CBD isolate: 979 vs 786 kcal at ad lib lunch; no change in subjective appetite.
  • RCTBornæs et al., Nutrients 2026 PMID 42514343 · DOISecondary analysis of crossover RCT (n=17, age 65+, poor appetite). Sativex: no significant change in appetite, GLP-1, ghrelin.

Studies suggest; they do not prove. Animal results may not apply to people. Not medical advice; talk to a doctor.
