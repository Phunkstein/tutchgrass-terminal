In plain words

Rats, 2026: a β-caryophyllene (BCP) nanoemulsion cut paw swelling, spared cartilage and lowered inflammatory markers (TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6).

Knee osteoarthritis, 2026 RCT: a 10 mg THC pill (dronabinol) didn't beat placebo on pain in 21 people.

Rats aren't people.

Studies (PubMed)
  • PRECLINICALChagas et al., J Pharm Pharmacol 2026 PMID 42814598 · DOIRat study. Nanoemulsion β-caryophyllene reduced paw swelling/arthritis score, preserved cartilage, lowered TNF-α/IL-1β/IL-6.
  • RCTHamilton et al., Anesthesiology 2026 PMID 41481837 · DOIHuman RCT (n=21 knee OA). Dronabinol 10 mg: no significant difference in clinical pain vs placebo.
