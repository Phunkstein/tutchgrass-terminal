  • Colorado MED's 2020 market update puts average tested THC at 19.17% for flower and 67.82% for concentrates (500 mg cartridges 79.67%). MED report ↗
  • A CU Boulder study (Scientific Reports, 2025) bought 277 Colorado dispensary products from 52 stores and retested them. Mean labeled THC for flower was 22.5%, but the lab measured 20.8%. Labels were more than 15% higher than the lab result on 30.3% of flower samples. Concentrates were within ±15% of label 96% of the time. Study ↗
  • Michigan: on Aug 20, 2026 the CRA announced a voluntary recall of Nuggz Nectar 200 mg infused drinks after testing showed they may exceed THC limits, with potential potency variability and an inaccurate serving count. CRA release ↗
  • Transparency gap: we checked the public websites of about 30 licensed Colorado and Michigan brands. Only one publicly posted COA (a Lume extract, via Dixie) turned up. Most licensed products' COAs sit behind the package QR code or the store counter.

The federal Dec 11 hemp per-container limit does not apply to state-licensed marijuana, so these products are never flagged for it.
