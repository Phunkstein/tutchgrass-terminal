Privacy

What we store. If you make an account: your email (handled by our auth provider, Supabase), your 21+ confirmation, an optional public username, your game scores, the brands you watch, and your submissions. If you join the Weekly COA drop: your email, your opt-in, your 21+ confirmation, the exact consent wording, when you signed up, and which page you signed up from.

What we don't store. Passwords (sign-in is by magic link or Google). We run no ad trackers and we don't sell data.

Who can see it. Emails are never public. Database row-level security means you can read only your own private rows. The username and game scores you choose to publish are public.

Email. The newsletter is double opt-in, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe link.

Your controls. You can delete your account from the Account page, which removes your profile, scores, watches and username. To erase newsletter or submission records, use the corrections form.

Local storage. The game keeps your streak in your browser's localStorage.

Errors are possible. Numbers are copied from brand-posted COAs and rounded to 1 decimal; always check the linked COA. Not medical or legal advice. Brands can request corrections. 21+.
