Privacy

What we store. If you make an account: your email (handled by our auth provider, Supabase), your 21+ confirmation, an optional public username, the conditions you pick, the studies, findings and conditions you save, and your email settings (new-study alerts and the weekly digest, on by default, with the time you agreed). Choices you make before signing up stay in your own browser until you sign in.

What we don't store. Payment details, health records, or anything about whether you use cannabis. Picking a condition is not a diagnosis, and we never share or sell your list.

Who can see it. Only you. Row-level security in our database limits every row to its owner.

Email. Alerts and the digest are sent through Brevo. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. You can also switch them off under My Condition or your account.

Delete. Your account page has a delete button. It removes your account and everything linked to it.
