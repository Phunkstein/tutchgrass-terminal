⏳ Federal hemp change · Dec 11, 2026
43days
--hrs
--min
--sec
Max 0.4 mg total THC per container for final hemp products. Synthetic-cannabinoid exclusion starts Nov 12 (14 days).

Key dates

  • Nov 12, 2025P.L. 119-37 (FY2026 Agriculture appropriations) rewrites the federal definition of hemp, effective in 365 days.
  • Sep 2, 2026Congress passes a continuing resolution, P.L. 119-103, delaying most of the new definition to Dec 11, 2026.
  • Nov 12, 2026Products with cannabinoids not capable of being naturally produced by the plant (certain synthetics) stop counting as hemp.
  • Dec 11, 2026The rest takes effect: total THC (including THCA) ≤ 0.3% dry weight, and final products over 0.4 mg combined total THC per container are excluded from hemp.

Sources: Congressional Research Service IF13136 ↗ · IN12620 ↗ · 7 U.S.C. §1639o ↗. Not legal advice.
