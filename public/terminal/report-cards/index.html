Method (short)

AI tools read each COA a brand published on its own site and copy the label claim and the lab's measured value. Accuracy = tested ÷ claim × 100; deviation = accuracy − 100. On label = within ±10%. Score = 100 − the average size of the deviation (never below 0). Grades: A+ ≥97, A ≥93, A− ≥90, B+ ≥87, B ≥83, B− ≥80, C+ ≥77, C ≥73, C− ≥70, D+ ≥67, D ≥63, D− ≥60, F below 60. Fewer than 3 scored COAs = small sample. COAs with no label number to compare are listed but not scored.

A grade describes label-vs-lab numbers only. It says nothing about safety, quality, legality or health effects. Not medical or legal advice. Brands can request a correction.
