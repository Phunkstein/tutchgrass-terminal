Aroma: earthy, musky, herbal · Also in: hops, mango, lemongrass
Human trialMyrcene

40-woman double-blind RCT: a CBD + myrcene gel cut vestibulodynia pain scores more than placebo over 60 days.

Studied: 5% CBD gel plus myrcene (a combo, so the myrcene effect can't be isolated)

Murina et al. · Biomedicines · 2025-10 · PMID 41153723 ↗
β-Caryophyllene7 papers in 18 mo
Aroma: peppery, spicy, woody · Also in: black pepper, cloves
Human studyβ-Caryophyllene

Patients who use cannabis for pain said they look for CBD and caryophyllene, and myrcene for pain + anxiety. These are preferences, not proof.

Studied: Survey + interviews of 1,060 medical cannabis patients

Fedorova et al. · J Psychoactive Drugs · 2025-07 · PMID 40605183 ↗
Reviewβ-Caryophyllene

Systematic review: CBD + β-caryophyllene looked synergistic for chronic pain, but every study so far was preclinical.

Studied: CBD + β-caryophyllene (systematic review of preclinical work)

Benamar · Phytomedicine · 2026-09 · PMID 42753475 ↗
