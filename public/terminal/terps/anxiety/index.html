Linalool5 papers in 18 mo
Aroma: floral, lavender · Also in: lavender, coriander
Human trialLinalool

58 adults with high blood pressure: 15 minutes a day of lavender oil inhalation cut anxiety scores compared with an almond-oil placebo (pilot RCT).

Studied: Lavender essential oil (linalool-rich), not cannabis

Wang et al. · Complement Ther Med · 2025-10 · PMID 41177368 ↗
Human trialLinalool

Dental surgery patients given lavender aromatherapy had lower heart rate and cortisol and needed less anesthetic (triple-blind RCT).

Studied: Lavender essential oil (linalool-rich), not cannabis

Argueta-Figueroa et al. · Dent Med Probl · 2026-01 · PMID 41553229 ↗
🔄 Plot twist
Lab / animalLinalool

Plot twist: in a mouse model of chemical sensitivity, inhaled linalool INCREASED anxiety-like behavior.

Studied: Inhaled linalool, mouse model of chemical sensitivity

Sato et al. · J Toxicol Sci · 2026-01 · PMID 42386544 ↗
Myrcene
Aroma: earthy, musky, herbal · Also in: hops, mango, lemongrass
Human studyMyrcene

Patients using cannabis for pain + anxiety reported preferring myrcene. The anxiety-only group was less likely to prefer terpinolene.

Studied: Survey of 1,060 medical cannabis patients

Fedorova et al. · J Psychoactive Drugs · 2025-07 · PMID 40605183 ↗
Pinene3 papers in 18 mo
Aroma: pine, fresh · Also in: pine needles, rosemary
Lab / animalPinene

α-Pinene reduced anxiety-like behavior and brain inflammation markers in rats.

Studied: Pure α-pinene, rats

Nasiri et al. · Acta Neuropsychiatr · 2026-04 · PMID 42025586 ↗
Humulene1 papers in 18 mo
Aroma: woody, hoppy · Also in: hops, sage
Lab / animalHumulene

α-Humulene had anti-anxiety-like effects in zebrafish, via serotonin 5-HT3 receptors rather than the Valium-type site.

Studied: Pure α-humulene, zebrafish

Borges et al. · Chem Biodivers · 2026-07 · PMID 42503214 ↗

Aroma: peppery, spicy, woody · Also in: black pepper, cloves

Lab / animalβ-Caryophyllene

Plot twist: β-caryophyllene did NOT change anxiety-like behavior in healthy mice, whether given once or daily.

Studied: Pure β-caryophyllene, healthy mice
