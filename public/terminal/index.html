Full listing for this guide

When the power goes out for days, your phone may be the only "library" you have. Make sure the right guide is already on it.

The Grid-Down Survival Guide is a practical, no-nonsense plan for regular people: no bunker, no wilderness expertise, and no expensive specialty gear required. It walks you through the first 72 hours, then helps you build toward two weeks and beyond, one affordable step at a time.

What's inside

  • What "grid-down" really means: the realistic scenarios and what actually stops working
  • The first 72 hours: a clear action plan for when the lights go out
  • Water: how much to store, how to store it, and exact boiling and bleach treatment methods based on CDC guidance
  • Food: a pantry you'll actually eat, a worked 30-day pantry example with quantities, and cooking safely without power
  • Power and lighting: batteries, solar, and generator safety to prevent carbon monoxide poisoning and shock
  • Shelter: staying warm, staying cool, and sheltering in place
  • Health: first aid, medications, and sanitation without normal plumbing
  • Communication: staying informed when networks fail
  • Protection: securing your home, money, and important documents
  • Stay or leave: deciding early, and planning for kids, older adults, and pets
  • Skills and recovery: what to practice now and how to work with neighbors
  • Prepare in stages: a budget-friendly plan so you can start with what you have

Printable tools included

  • Master readiness checklist
  • Household plan and emergency contacts worksheet
  • Inventory and resource planner
  • Sources and quick-reference index

Why this guide

  • Built for offline use. Tiny file (about 0.1 MB), no photos, and easy to read on a phone screen.
  • Grounded in trusted sources. Key facts come from FEMA/Ready.gov, the CDC, the American Red Cross, and the EPA, with sources listed in the back.
  • Easy to navigate. Clickable table of contents, color-coded sections, and clear warning boxes so you can find things fast under stress.
  • Practical, not fearful. Calm, step-by-step guidance with worked examples you can adapt to your household.

Format

25-page PDF (US Letter), instant download. Read it on any phone, tablet, or computer, or print the checklists for your kit.

Tip: After you buy, save the PDF to your phone and open it once in airplane mode so you know it'll be there when you need it.
