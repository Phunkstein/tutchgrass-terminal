Linalool: The Calm Florist

Lavender-soft and unbothered. You are everyone's chill friend.

Aroma: floral, lavender · Also found in: lavender, coriander

What studies looked at: 10 recent studies we read mention Linalool for anxiety, inflammation, mood, nausea, pain, sleep; 3 were in humans. Example: 80 heart-failure patients: those who inhaled lavender oil nightly for 4 weeks reported better sleep than a placebo-oil group (open-label RCT). PMID 41499426 ↗
